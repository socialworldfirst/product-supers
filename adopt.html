Product Super pack

This is the WorldFirst Product Super pack: the rules, a starter project, the assets, an adoption guide and a rule checker. It is encrypted. The password comes from the WorldFirst social team and is not on this page.

Open it with one command, replacing PASSWORD:

curl -fsSL https://worldfirstinternal.org/product-supers/pack.enc | openssl enc -d -aes-256-cbc -pbkdf2 -iter 200000 -md sha256 -pass pass:PASSWORD | tar xz

It creates a folder named product-supers. Read ADOPT.md first.

See the supers in motion (same password). Plain-text version of this page: llms.txt.
